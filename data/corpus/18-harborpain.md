Synthetic leaflet for fictional Harborpain 200 mg tablets. This is not a real medication label. The leaflet says not more than 6 tablets in 24 hours. Take Harborpain with food. The Harborpain leaflet says people with a stomach ulcer, kidney disease, or a Harborpain allergy should not use it and should talk with a clinician. Children under 12 are not covered. This leaflet does not give a personal dose.
