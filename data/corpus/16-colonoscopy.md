title: Colonoscopy preparation handout
---

Harborlight uses a fictional prep called Cedarhaven Clear Prep. This colonoscopy handout is synthetic and is not a personal plan. On the day before the colonoscopy, drink clear liquids only after 10:00, and drink no red or purple liquids. At 16:00 drink the first half of Cedarhaven Clear Prep, 8 ounces every 15 minutes. At 20:00 drink the second half. Have nothing by mouth after midnight except the prep. A driver must take the patient home. Do not drive yourself home after the colonoscopy.
